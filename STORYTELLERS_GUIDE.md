# 📖 RimWatch Storytellers Guide

RimWatch includes 5 AI personalities, each with unique decision-making styles. Choose the one that matches your playstyle!

## 🛡️ Cautious Strategist

**Philosophy:** *"Better safe than sorry"*

### Personality Traits
- **Risk Tolerance:** 20% - Avoids dangerous situations
- **Building Speed:** 40% - Slow, methodical construction
- **Trade Aggressiveness:** 30% - Conservative trading
- **Defense Style:** DEFENSIVE - Focuses on walls and killboxes
- **Research Priority:** DEFENSIVE - Prioritizes armor and medicine
- **Social Focus:** HIGH - Keeps colonists happy

### Best For
- ✅ Beginners learning RimWorld
- ✅ Permadeath runs
- ✅ Challenging biomes (ice sheets, deserts)
- ✅ Players who hate losing colonists

### Playstyle
- Builds strong defenses before expanding
- Stockpiles food and medicine
- Avoids risky raids and quests
- Focuses on colonist well-being

### Crafting Strategy
- **Weapon Focus:** Ranged weapons (rifles, charge rifles)
- **Armor Priority:** Devilstrand, flak armor
- **Production:** Conservative, stockpiles essentials

## ⚖️ Balanced Manager

**Philosophy:** *"The golden mean"*

### Personality Traits
- **Risk Tolerance:** 50% - Calculated risks
- **Building Speed:** 60% - Steady expansion
- **Trade Aggressiveness:** 50% - Opportunistic trading
- **Defense Style:** BALANCED - Mix of active and passive
- **Research Priority:** BALANCED - All-around progress
- **Social Focus:** MEDIUM - Maintains stability

### Best For
- ✅ Normal difficulty runs
- ✅ Players who want "vanilla+" experience
- ✅ Balanced biomes (temperate forests)
- ✅ First-time autopilot users

### Playstyle
- Expands at steady pace
- Balances all colony needs
- Takes calculated risks
- Adapts to situations

### Crafting Strategy
- **Weapon Focus:** Mixed (melee + ranged)
- **Armor Priority:** Marine armor
- **Production:** Balanced stockpiles

---

## ⚔️ Aggressive Conqueror

**Philosophy:** *"The best defense is a good offense"*

### Personality Traits
- **Risk Tolerance:** 80% - Embraces danger
- **Building Speed:** 80% - Rapid expansion
- **Trade Aggressiveness:** 70% - Active trading
- **Defense Style:** OFFENSIVE - Mobile strike teams
- **Research Priority:** OFFENSIVE - Weapons and bionics
- **Social Focus:** LOW - Efficiency over happiness

### Best For
- ✅ Experienced players
- ✅ Rich biomes (tropical rainforests)
- ✅ Players who love combat
- ✅ Fast-paced runs

### Playstyle
- Expands aggressively
- Seeks out raids and quests
- Prioritizes wealth generation
- Builds powerful colonists

### Crafting Strategy
- **Weapon Focus:** Heavy weapons (miniguns, charge lances)
- **Armor Priority:** Cataphract, marine armor
- **Production:** Focuses on trade goods and weapons

---

## 🎲 Chaotic Experimenter

**Philosophy:** *"Why not try everything?"*

### Personality Traits
- **Random every decision!**
- **Building Speed:** 10-90% - Completely unpredictable
- **Trade Aggressiveness:** 10-90% - May hoard or dump everything
- **Defense Style:** RANDOM - Changes constantly
- **Research Priority:** RANDOM - Whatever seems interesting
- **Social Focus:** RANDOM - Chaos reigns

### Best For
- ✅ Players bored with meta strategies
- ✅ Challenge runs
- ✅ Streamers wanting entertaining content
- ✅ Masochists 😈

### Playstyle
- Absolutely unpredictable
- May build 20 bedrooms or none
- Could research art before medicine
- Assigns random work priorities

### Crafting Strategy
- **Everything is random!**
- May craft 100 wooden clubs
- Might prioritize art over food
- Pure chaos mode

⚠️ **Warning:** This storyteller can (and will) get your colonists killed in creative ways!

## 🎨 Custom Storyteller

**Philosophy:** *"Your way"*

### Customizable Traits
Configure every aspect:

```
🎚️ Risk Tolerance: [0% - 100%]
🎚️ Building Speed: [0% - 100%]
🎚️ Trade Aggressiveness: [0% - 100%]

🎯 Defense Style: Defensive / Balanced / Offensive
🔬 Research Priority: Defensive / Balanced / Offensive / Economic
👥 Social Focus: Low / Medium / High
```

### Crafting Strategy
```
⚔️ Weapon Focus: Melee / Ranged / Heavy / Mixed
🛡️ Armor Priority: Light / Medium / Heavy
📦 Production Style: Conservative / Balanced / Aggressive
```

### Best For
- ✅ Players who know exactly what they want
- ✅ Specific challenge runs
- ✅ Testing different strategies
- ✅ Fine-tuning AI behavior

### Creating Custom Profiles

1. **Open Profile Manager**
   - RimWatch Panel → Settings → "Manage Profiles"

2. **Adjust Sliders**
   - Set each trait to desired value
   - Test in-game
   - Iterate until perfect

3. **Save & Share**
   - Save profile with descriptive name
   - Export to XML
   - Share with community!

### Example Custom Profiles

## 🔄 Random Storyteller

**Philosophy:** *"Variety is the spice of life"*

### How It Works
- Switches between Cautious, Balanced, and Aggressive
- Changes every 5-10 in-game days
- Smooth transitions (no sudden chaos)

### Best For
- ✅ Long-term playthroughs
- ✅ Players who want variety
- ✅ Testing all storyteller styles
- ✅ Unpredictable but not chaotic

### Personality Traits
- Inherits traits from current active storyteller
- May switch mid-crisis!
- Keeps you on your toes

## 🎯 Choosing the Right Storyteller

### Ask Yourself:

**1. What's your experience level?**
- Beginner → **Cautious** or **Balanced**
- Intermediate → **Balanced** or **Aggressive**
- Expert → **Aggressive** or **Chaotic**

**2. What's your goal?**
- Survive → **Cautious**
- Build impressive colony → **Balanced**
- Conquer the Rim → **Aggressive**
- Have fun → **Chaotic** or **Random**

**3. How much control do you want?**
- Full autopilot → **Balanced**
- Specific strategy → **Custom**
- Surprise me → **Random** or **Chaotic**

## 🔧 Switching Storytellers Mid-Game

**Yes, you can change storytellers anytime!**

1. Open RimWatch Panel
2. Settings tab → "Change Storyteller"
3. Select new storyteller
4. Click "Apply"

### Tips for Switching
- ✅ Can switch as often as you want
- ✅ AI adapts to new personality immediately
- ⚠️ Mid-construction plans may change
- ⚠️ Expect different priorities

## 💡 Pro Tips

### Cautious Strategist
- Let it build defenses before triggering raids
- Don't force expansion—it knows when it's ready
- Trust the food stockpiling

### Balanced Manager
- Most "set and forget" option
- Great for learning AI behavior
- Least likely to surprise you

### Aggressive Conqueror
- Keep medicine stocked—colonists get injured
- Monitor wealth—attracts bigger raids
- Have backup colonists ready

### Chaotic Experimenter
- Save often!
- Monitor closely
- Prepare for anything
- Have fun with the chaos

### Custom Storyteller
- Start with presets, then tweak
- Test in dev mode first
- Document successful profiles
- Share with community!
